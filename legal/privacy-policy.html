1. Introduction

Welcome to LetsMakeMusic ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LetsMakeMusic (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, password (encrypted), phone number
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile photo, bio
  • User-Generated Content: Songs, lyrics, artwork, and other creative content

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Location Data: With your permission, approximate location at signup and login
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, features used, time spent
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, app crashes and errors

2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

If you sign in using third-party services, we may receive:

  • Apple Sign-In: Name, email address, unique identifier
  • Google Sign-In: Name, email address, profile photo, unique identifier
  • Facebook Login: Name, email address, profile photo, unique identifier
  • Phone Authentication: Phone number for SMS verification

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's features
  • Generate AI-powered music, lyrics, and artwork
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Send push notifications about your content and updates
  • Personalize your experience and provide recommendations
  • Communicate with you about updates and support
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where required for legal purposes.

We implement appropriate security measures including encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, access controls, and regular security assessments. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.
